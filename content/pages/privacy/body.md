**This build sets no cookies, loads no third-party scripts and stores no submissions.** Fonts, images and animation files are served from this site's own origin. Analytics, when added, will be consent-gated and this page will be updated first.

**Inquiry form.** When the inquiry service is connected, a submission is validated and sent as an email to the company mailbox; the website keeps no copy. Email providers and mailboxes retain messages under their own policies.

**Server logs.** The web server keeps standard access logs (IP address, requested URL, time, user agent) for security and capacity purposes.
